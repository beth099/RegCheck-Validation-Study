Emotion Perception and Extrinsic Regulation in Couples

OSF ID: t6jcv | Registered: 2023-01-25T22:12:51.498583 | Showing: original version (1 version(s) on OSF)

Study Information

Hypotheses

It is expected that emotional clarity and range of emotional experiences would predict the emotion perception accuracy of the regulator. It is expected that the emotion perception accuracy of the regulator will predict the efficiency of extrinsic regulation.

Study design

We will recruit both members of couple and assign them into one of two roles (regulator or target). Each will read 4 vignettes and rate the intensity of either their own emotions (targets) or rate the perceived intensity of their partners' emotions (regulators) if they were in the situations described. Regulators will indicate what they would say to the partner. Targets will indicate they would want their partner to say to them to regulate their emotions. We will calculate regulators' accuracy as the correspondence between their perceived intensity ratings and the targets' ratings. The efficiency of the extrinsic regulation will be assessed by the similarity of open-ended responses for the regulator's strategies and the target's preferred strategies rated by coders.

Randomization

We will use simple randomization for assigning participants to one of two roles: regulator or target.

Explanation of existing data

Data collection procedures

The study will use an online survey format and will be conducted via Prolific. Couples in which both partners agree to participate in the study will be provided with the survey link created on Qualtrics. Each participant will be compensated with 3.50 USD upon their completion of the survey.

Sample size

We aim to run at least 200 participants (100 couples).

Sample size rationale

Based on previous similar studies and the limited budget allocated to this survey, we hope to collect at least 100 couples.

Measured variables

Emotion perception accuracy will be measured by the similarity between the ratings from the target and regulator. The efficiency of extrinsic regulation will be coded using the match between open-ended answers of the participants for regulation strategies by independent coders.

Indices

Emotional clarity will be assessed by the Trait Meta-Mood Scale -24 Clarity of Feelings Subscale (Salovey et al., 1995) and the range of emotional experiences will be measured by the Range and Differentiation of Emotional Experience Scale / Range Subscale (Kang &amp; Shaver, 2004).

Analysis Plan

Statistical models

We will use multiple regression to test for a mediational model between emotional clarity and a range of emotional experiences as predictors, emotion perception accuracy as a mediator, and extrinsic regulation efficiency as the outcome.
