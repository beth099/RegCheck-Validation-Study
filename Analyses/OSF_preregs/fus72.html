Nostalgia--Skepticism about Change, Social Connectedness--Atittudes Toward Innovative Technology (Correlational)

OSF ID: fus72 | Registered: 2022-02-21T02:08:17.594542

Study Information

Hypotheses

We hypothesize that skepticism about change will mediate negatively (inhibit) the relation between trait nostalgia and atittudes toward innovative technology, whereas social connectedness will mediate positively (facilitate) the relation between trait nostalgia and atittudes toward innovative technology.

Study design

I. The predictor is trait nostalgia. We will assess this construct with the 7-item version of the Southampton Nostalgia Scale provided below. According to the Oxford Dictionary, ‘nostalgia’ is defined as a ‘sentimental longing for the past.’ 1. How valuable is nostalgia for you? 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Not at all Very much 2. How important is it for you to bring to mind nostalgic experiences? 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Not at all Very much 3. How significant is it for you to feel nostalgic? 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Not at all Very much 4. How prone are you to feeling nostalgic? 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Not at all Very much 5. How often do you experience nostalgia? 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Very rarely Very frequently 6. Generally speaking, how often do you bring to mind nostalgic experiences? 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Very rarely Very frequently 7. Specifically, how often do you bring to mind nostalgic experiences? (Please check one.) _____ Once or twice a year _____ Once every couple of months _____ Once or twice a month _____ Approximately twice a week _____ Approximately once a week _____ Three to four times a week _____ At least once a day II. One presumed mediator is skepticism about change. We will assess this construct with four items that we generated for the purposes of this study: -I am not sure change is good. -Change can lead you to places you don’t want to go. -Change can be tricky. -I am skeptical about change. Response scale: 1 = strongly disagree, 7 = strongly agree. III. Another presumed mediator is social connectedness. We will assess this construct with the relevant 4 items of the Nostalgia Functions Scale (Hepper et al., 2012, Emotion): In general, I feel … ___ connected to loved ones ___ protected ___ loved ___ I can trust others Response scale: 1 = strongly disagree, 7 = strongly agree. IV. The outcomes are support for research on AI technology and support for research on 5G technology. We will assess the former construct with three items: “To what extent do you support research on AI technology?”, “To what extent do you support the use of taxpayer money for research on AI technology?”, and “To what extent do you support increasing state funding for research on AI technology? (1 = not at all, 7 = very much). We will assess the latter construct with the same three items: “To what extent do you support research on 5G technology?”, “To what extent do you support the use of taxpayer money for research on 5G technology?”, and “To what extent do you support increasing state funding for research on 5G technology? (1 = not at all, 7 = very much).

Randomization

We will administer the measures in the following order: nostalgia scale first, followed by the skepticism about change and social connectedness scales, and ending with the attitudes toward innovative technology scales (two AI items, two 5G items). We will counterbalance the order of (1) the skepticism about change and social connectedness scales, and (2) the AI and 5G items.

Data collection procedures

We will collect data from UK participants via Prolific Academic. The survey links will be set active until we obtain the intended sample size. We will remunerate each participant with 1 GBP (1.39 USD).

Sample size

To obtain stable correlations, we will recruit at least 250 participants (Schönbrodt &amp; Perugini, 2013). In anticipation of attrition, we will conservatively recruit 300 participants.

Analysis Plan

Statistical models

We will conduct regression analyses using the PROCESS macro or Mplus to test the mediating roles of social connectedness and skepticism about change in the relation between nostalgia and support for research on innovative technology.
