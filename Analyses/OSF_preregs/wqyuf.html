Study Information

Title

Jumping-to-Conclusions Error, Belief, and Knowledge Corruption

Authors

Carmen Sanchez, David Alan Dunning

Description

The study is designed to test whether a cognitive tendency (jumping-to-conclusions) that predicts delusion-proneness among schizophrenia patients also predicts errors and biases in the general public. This study is a direct replication of a study that we ran that was not pre-registered. We make identical predications as we made in our previous study that is under review.

Hypotheses

We will correlate JTC with all our dependent measures. We predict more belief bias, more overconfidence (confidence – accuracy), more denominator neglect, and lower scores on the CRT for high jumpers.
