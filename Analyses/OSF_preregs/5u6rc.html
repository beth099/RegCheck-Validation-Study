Study Information

Hypotheses

The main question relates to the comparison of mean RT of task 1 vs. the mean RT of task 2 (i.e., averaging across color and letter task conditions). If participants can quickly reallocate freed-up resources after they have finished limited processing of task 1, mean RT of task 2 should be less than mean RT of task 1 at long SOA. 1. In order to consider task order, we will conduct a mixed ANOVA with the within-subject factor of task (task 1 vs. task 2) and the between-subject factor of task order (task 1=color/task 2=letter vs. task 1=letter/task2=color) for trials with long SOA. We predict a main effect of task with faster mean RTs for task 2 compared with task 1. For completeness and in order to trace out the time course of the potential RT1 vs. RT2 difference, we will also conduct and report a 2x2x2 mixed measures ANOVA with the within-subject factors task (task 1 vs. task 2), SOA (50 vs 1000 ms) and the between-subject factor of task order (task 1=color/task 2=letter vs. task 1=letter/task2=color).
