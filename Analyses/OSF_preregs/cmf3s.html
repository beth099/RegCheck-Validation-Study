Clause structure as a cue for discourse relations (coherence judgment)

OSF ID: cmf3s | Registered: 2022-07-12T08:52:32.626452

Study Information

Hypotheses

RQ1) Are GFA clauses preferred more in a result relation compared to a detail relation? H0: There is no difference in the preference for GFA’s in result and detail relations. H1: GFA clauses are preferred more in result relations than in detail relations.

Study design

Participants will take part in a forced-choice judgment task with two alternatives. The task has a within-subjects and between-item design. The task contains 22 experimental items in two conditions: detail and result. The items are distributed over two lists so that each item is presented only once in one of the conditions to participants. The order of item presentation will be automatically randomized per participant.

Stopping rule

Variables

Manipulated variables

The discourse relation is the manipulated variable. It is either a result relation or a detail relation. This was achieved by changing the content of the second clause, while keeping the first clause and the tense the same across conditions. The items have been pretested to ensure that the target interpretation was dominant (&gt;60% preferred the target interpretation over the other relation).

Measured variables

The dependent variable is the paraphrase that the participant selects. These paraphrases differ in the structure of the first clause. This clause is either a full matrix clause or a gerund free adjunct.

Analysis Plan

Statistical models

We will analyze whether the selected paraphrase is dependent on the relation sense using a logistic mixed-effects regression analysis. We will use a maximal random effect structure, reducing it only in case of non-convergence. To summarize, our model specification is as follows: M &lt;- glmer(paraphrase == GFA ~ relationsense + (1 + relationsense | itemid) + (1 + relationsense | workerid)

Transformations

Inference criteria

We will use the standard alpha-level of 0.05 to determine significance. P-values are provided by the package lmerTest.

Data exclusion

We will use 4 questions as attention check. For these items, the participants are explicitly instructed which option to select in the context. Participant who fail to adhere to this instruction more than once will be excluded and replaced
