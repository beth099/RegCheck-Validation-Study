Dehumanization - Values 2

OSF ID: p3r8n | Registered: 2020-10-09T01:51:04.148810

Study Information

Hypotheses

1. Perceivers will dehumanize less attractive- and less intelligent-looking targets. 2. Perceivers' dehumanization of male targets will depend more on the men's perceived intelligence than their attractiveness. 3. Perceivers' dehumanization of female targets will depend more on the women's attractiveness than their perceived intelligence. 4. Perceivers' higher weighting of intelligence vs. attractiveness in male/female targets (for dehumanization) will be predicted by their higher ranking of intelligence and attractiveness, respectively.

Study design

Between subjects design with 1 factor (gender) and 2 levels (male vs. female) Participants will first rate 206 male or 206 female targets (counterbalanced) on the blatant dehumanization scale. Targets will be randomized within blocks. Afterwards, participants will be asked to rank how important 12 traits are for men or women to possess (depending on which gender they rated in the dehumanization block). To be comprehensive, 10 of the traits span the Big Five personality traits (2 items per trait) and the final 2 traits are physical attractiveness and intelligence. Afterwards, participants will report their race, sex, age, SES, country of residence, whether they recognized any of the targets, and whether the photos loaded properly for them.

Randomization

Block randomization via Qualtrics software (rate 206 male or 206 female faces)
