Study Information

Hypotheses

H1 (Primary): Conservative participants will be more likely than liberal participants to show a pro-White/anti-Black shift in hiring recommendations when in the pro-diversity (vs. neutral) condition. In other words: A 2-way interaction between the Diversity Manipulation and Political Orientation H2 (Secondary): White conservatives will be more likely than BIPOC conservatives to show a pro-White/anti-Black shift in hiring recommendations when in the pro-diversity (vs. neutral) condition. In other words: In the 3-way interaction (Diversity Manipulation x Political Orientation x Participant Race), simple effects of the diversity manipulation will be larger for conservative Whites than for conservative BIPOC participants. H3 (Exploratory): Political symbolic ideology will demonstrate a larger moderating effect of diversity condition than operational ideology.

Study design

Participants (U.S. Prolific users) will be asked to enter their unique Prolific ID, then will answer demographic items followed by consenting to participating in the study. After, they will learn about a company that either does or does not have diversity messages in their website materials. Participants will then view and rate the qualifications of 4 candidates applying for a job at the company. One candidate will have a Latina-sounding name and be much more qualified than the other participants. One candidate will have a white male sounding name and be much less qualified than the other participants. Two candidates will have matched qualifications (not exactly the same, but rated as the same in pre-studies). One of these candidates will have a black male sounding name, and the other will have a white male sounding name. After rating the candidates (qualifications, positivity, other's positivity, competence) in a counterbalanced order, participants will rank the candidates from most preferred to last preferred. They will also rank the importance of each form of qualification (education, experience, resume, interview) from most important to least important. Participants will then be randomly assigned to learn that after the highly qualified Latina turned down the org's offer of employment, they decided to hire either the matched white male candidate or the matched black male candidate. Participants will then rank this candidate's anticipated job performance and how fair they believed the decision to be. After, participants will be asked about their general feelings about the organization and fill-out several individual difference measures to be assessed as potential moderators (rwa, symbolic ideology, operational ideology). Finally, participants will answer 5 manipulation check items. Please see attached doc for exact wording and stimuli

Randomization

- Randomizing exposure to diversity messages vs. diversity-neutral messages (simple, between subjects) - Randomizing whether participants learn the black or white matched candidate is hired (simple, between subjects) - Randomizing order in which 4 candidates are viewed and rated (simple, within-subjects) - Randomizing whether participants see the white candidate with matched qualifications 1 and the black candidate with matched qualifications 2 (or vice-versa; simple, between subjects).

Explanation of existing data

Data collection procedures

Data will be collected from Prolific. U.S.-based Prolific users who identify as either White, Black or African American, Latin or Hispanic, or Asian or Asian American will be eligible to view and participate in the study. Participants will be paid $2.00 for the 10-minute study. Participants will be excluded if they do not answer any dependent measures, if they fail either of the 2 attention checks, if they indicate they want their anonymous data destroyed after debriefing, or if they spend less than 10 seconds looking at the diversity manipulation stimuli.

Sample size

N = 2100 n(White) = 1050 n(BIPOC) = 1050

Sample size rationale

For a 2 (diversity manipulation) x Continuous (Conservatism) design, an a priori power analysis (using the R package, InteractionPoweR and effect size from previous study) revealed a sample size of 2100 to have 82% power to detect a small (f = .06) effect size. To have a well-balanced sample size, 1050 responses will be collected from White participants, broken down further into 400 liberals and 650 moderates or conservatives to achieve a normal distribution by political ideology. 1050 responses will be collected from BIPOC participants, also broken down further into 400 liberals, and 650 moderates or conservatives to achieve a normal distribution by political ideology. Because there were not enough BIPOC conservative participants available on Prolific, conservatives and moderates were grouped together.

Stopping rule

We will stop when we have collected 2100 valid responses

Analysis Plan

Statistical models

Prior to testing primary hypotheses, we will use a 3-way mixed ANOVA with the diversity manipulation as a between-subjects factor, participant race as a between-subjects factor, and candidate (black or white matched) as a within-subjects factor. The outcome variable will be candidate rating. Significant or marginal (p &lt; .10, two-sided) interactions will be followed-up with simple slopes assessments. Similar analyses will be conducted on our other dependent variables, with some necessary modifications (moving to a logistic regression framework for the ranking variable, adding the hiring manipulation as a between-subjects factor for the DVs that occur after that manipulation). Hypothesis 1 [H1 (Primary): Conservative participants will be more likely than liberal participants to show a pro-White/anti-Black shift in hiring recommendations when in the pro-diversity (vs. neutral) condition. In other words: A 2-way interaction between the Diversity Manipulation and Political Orientation] &amp; Hypothesis 2 [H2 (Secondary): White conservatives will be more likely than BIPOC conservatives to show a pro-White/anti-Black shift in hiring recommendations when in the pro-diversity (vs. neutral) condition. In other words: In the 3-way interaction (Diversity Manipulation x Political Orientation x Participant Race), simple effects of the diversity manipulation will be larger for conservative Whites than for conservative BIPOC participants]: A difference score between white and black candidate ratings will be computed in order to simplify analyses and allow us to use a regression framework. Then, moderated regression analysis will be used to test a 3-way interaction between the diversity manipulation, participant race, and participant political orientation. Specifically, we will enter the three main effects, the three 2-way interactions (cross-products), and the single 3-way interaction (cross-product). We will assess the diversity manipulation x political orientation 2-way interaction to assess H1 and the 3-way interaction to assess H3. Significant or marginal (p &lt; .10, two-sided) interactions will be followed-up with simple slopes assessments, but we will compare the simple effects of the diversity manipulation between conservative Whites and BIPOCs regardless of significance in order to test H2. Similar analyses will be conducted on our other dependent variables, with some necessary modifications (moving to a logistic regression framework for the ranking variable, adding the hiring manipulation and its interaction terms for the DVs that occur after that manipulation). Hypothesis 3 [H3: (Exploratory): Political symbolic ideology will demonstrate a larger moderating effect of diversity condition than operational ideology]: We will substitute-in symbolic then operational ideology for the political orientation variable in analyses described above. We will not statistically describe the difference in predictive utility of the two exploratory variables, but we will descriptively assess effect size differences to see whether one seems to interact more strongly with the diversity manipulation than the other.

Transformations

Mplus code 'center VAR(grandmean)' will be used to center the following variables: diversity manipulation, hiring manipulation, race, conservatism measures. Interaction variables will be created in Mplus to test 3- and 4-way interactions. Categorical outcome variables will be specified in Mplus code with 0 = favoring the White candidate and 1 = favoring the Black candidate.
