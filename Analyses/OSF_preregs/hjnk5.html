Sans Forgetica - Time on Task

OSF ID: hjnk5 | Registered: 2020-12-27T21:15:06.836061

Study Information

Hypotheses

I predict an interaction wherein self-paced study will engender a Sans Forgetica effect (SF &gt; Arial), but not when time is constrained (SF = Arial). I Accuracy Self paced: SF &gt; Arial Constrained: SF = Arial JOLs Arial &gt; SF

Data collection procedures

Collect participants on MTurK Inclusion: Location: US Learned English before age of 4 Hit rate 80% or higher # of hits approved: 50 or greater Exclusion: Taking the experiment(s) multiple times will result in only the first time being used Reports of program error Not finishing 99-100% of the experiment.

Sample size

232 participants will be run (116 per group). We will collect more just in case participants have to be thrown out due to the above criteria above.

Sample size rationale

This is based an a prior power analysis to detect a medium size effect (Geller, Davis, &amp; Peterson, 202).

Inference criteria

We will use the standard p&lt;.05 criteria for determining if the ANOVA and planned comparisons are significant different from those expected if the null hypothesis were correct. Null findings will be followed up with a Bayesian Analysis (using BFs) comparing main effects to interactions (how much evidence for one vs. the other) and also to examine the amount of evidence for planned contrasts.
