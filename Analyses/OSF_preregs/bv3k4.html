Study Information

Hypotheses

Hypothesis 1: We hypothesize that the participants in the high demand bias condition report a stronger increase in negative affect than the participants in the low demand bias condition. Hypothesis 2: We hypothesize that participants in the high demand bias condition report fewer or as many action urges than participants in the low demand bias condition.

Study design

2 x 2 x 4 mixed design: the between subject factor group (affect induction and affect assessment seperated vs. not seperated), the within subject factor time (affect assessment before and after the affect induction), and the between subject factor emotion (anger vs. anxiety vs. sadness vs. happiness)

Randomization

First, participants will be randomly assigned to one of the two experimental groups. This randomization will be done using the SosciSurvey survey platform. Within these groups, another randomization to a specific emotion induction is carried out in SosciSurvey.

Data collection procedures

Participants will be recruited through Prolific (https://www.prolific.co/), an online research platform. Inclusion criteria are a minimum age of 18 years, an approval rate of 90% or higher, and a completion of five or more previous studies. Participants will receive £2.60 for their participation based on a planned duration of 17 minutes, which is based on the minimum wage of £9.20 in UK in 2022

Sample size

The target sample size is 680 participants.

Sample size rationale

Due to the large number conditions, we take a pragmatic approach and base the planned sample size on the financial resources available to us. Thus, we will recruit 680 participants via Prolific (https://www.prolific.co/), which equals the cell size of Study 1. This sample size allows us to detect effect sizes of f = 0.16 or η^2 = .025 based on an alpha error of .05, a power of .95, and a numerator df of 3.

Stopping rule

Variables

Manipulated variables

Factor 1: Demand bias: Manipulation of the demand bias will take place by framing affect induction and affect assessment as part of one single study (high demand condition) vs. as part of two different subsequent studies (low demand condition). Factor 2: Induced emotion: Anger vs. anxiety vs. sadness vs. happiness will be manipulated using autobiographical recall (Mills et al. 2014).

Measured variables

- Sociodemographic variables (age, gender, education, country of residence) - Anger, anxiety, sadness and happiness measured via an adapted version of the Discrete Emotions Questionnaire (DEQ; Harmon-Jones et al., 2016), Item stem: Right now, I feel... Items: Anger: Angry, Mad, Pissed off; Anxiety: Anxious, Nervous, Worried; Sadness: Downhearted, Depressed, Sad; Happiness: Happy, Relaxed, Satisfied - Twenty Statement Test (TST; Kuhn &amp; McPartland, 1954) - Personality traits measured with BFI-2 (Soto &amp; John, 2017) - Mindfulness measured with the Multidimensional State Mindfulness Questionnaire (MSMQ; Blanke &amp; Brose, 2017) - Social Desirability Short Scale, English Version (KSE-G; Nießen et al., 2019)

Hypothesis 3 will be tested by included one personality moderator variable along with their two- and three-way interactions. Inference criteria

To evaluate if the multilevel model and post hoc test indicate that the results are significantly different from what would be expected if the null hypothesis were correct, the standard criterion p &lt;. 05 will be used. The Šidák correction will be applied to adjust for multiple comparisons.

Data exclusion

We will exclude participants who did not complete the experiment, failed the attention check, or have a degraded time index of greater than 100.

Missing data

Settings on SosciSurvey only allow participants to proceed when all items are completed. We will exclude all participants who did not finish the experiment.
