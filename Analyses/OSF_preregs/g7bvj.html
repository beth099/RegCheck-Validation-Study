Emotional entitlement

Study Information

Hypotheses

The scale will contain three factors: entitlement for negative emotion, entitlement for positive emotion and emotional revenge . The three factors will correlate with loneliness, life satisfaction, interpersonal emotion regulation, positive and negative emotion and the general scale of entitlement.

Analysis Plan

Statistical models

First, exploratory factor analysis will be conducted in order to explore the structure of the emotional entitlement scale. Second, multivariate analysis of variance (MANOVA) and Pearson correlations will be conducted in order to examine associations between the research variables. Finally to further explore the associations between the variables regression and mediation and moderation models will be conducted through SPSS and Process programs.
